About Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc.

Founded in 1927 in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental, Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. (KCC) is a Christ-centered learning community guided by the motto "Teach Ye All Nations."

  • Vision: A Christ-centered learning community of accountable and socially responsible individuals building the future and serving the Diocese, the nation, and the world.
  • Mission: To provide quality, inclusive Catholic education rooted in the Gospel, driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagements.
  • Core Pillars: Academic Excellence, Faith Formation, Community Service, and Holistic Development.

Why Choose Kabankalan Catholic College?

Since 1927, KCC has formed students into Christ-centered, academically competent, and socially responsible leaders ready to serve the Church, the community, and the nation.

Guided by our motto "Teach Ye All Nations," we nurture both mind and spirit through quality Catholic education, supportive campus life, and hands-on service that empower students to lead with faith, integrity, and compassion.
